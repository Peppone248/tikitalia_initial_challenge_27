MTAH27 · model history · 29 October → 4 November 2026

From the floor to the referee's whistle

In one week the spellF1 on the degraded IDSSE data, the same for all versions, went from 0.210 of the baseline to 0.5895 of the H/A/D HMM. This page goes through every stage with its numbers and also keeps count of what did not work or was set aside.

Read this first: where this history stops

It stops at the morning of 04/11. Later that day combined_algorithm (combined_algorithm/REPORT_COMBINED.md) moved to a new benchmark of 10 external matches, our own tracking and gradient-boosted trees. Its numbers are not comparable with the ones here, and that report finds that the degraded scenario used below is clearly not like the provided match.

Where we stood at this snapshot: two rankings that do not agree

  • On IDSSE, where by rule we make the decisions, the best is the H/A/D HMM: spellF1 0.5895 (degraded) and 0.7137 (clean).
  • On the public window (17 possessions, noise about ±0.39) the highest score overall belongs to the team model with the referee's whistle (R7a): spellF1 0.6963 and macroF1 0.9109.
  • The whistle cannot be measured on IDSSE, because those matches have no audio. So its strength is shown only by independent checks and by the public window, which is not enough to choose.

The path in one chart

The stages

  1. 0
    29/10 · THE FLOOR

    The official baseline

    The player closest to the ball has possession, then a majority vote over a window. Four variants of the same algorithm, only the window changes. The published floor is baseline_smooth50.

    0.0486 / 0.6532raw (spellF1 / macroF1)
    0.1996 / 0.6752smooth25
    0.2433 / 0.6724smooth50, the floor
    0.2242 / 0.6356smooth125
    0.210 / 0.462baseline50 on degraded IDSSE

    What it teaches

    • macroF1 moves by 0.04 over the whole sweep, spellF1 by a factor of 5: the two indicators must not be read the same way.
    • baseline50 on IDSSE (0.210) becomes the reference of all comparisons, since 0.2433 can be measured only on BAR-ATH.

    Warning

    • On the public window smooth125 wins, on the whole match smooth50: choosing on the 5 minutes picks the wrong variant.
  2. 1
    29–30/10 · HMM v0

    The first engine: 16 rules, a network and a Bakis HMM

    Rules that vote H, A, D or X, noise measured with the MAD, a light neural network trained on pseudo-labels of the rules and a five-state HMM (H, A, D, XH, XA) with minimum durations. No human label.

    0.289 / 0.642degraded IDSSE
    +0.079over baseline50, 7 matches out of 7
    0.369 / 0.720public window, 22 possessions vs 17
    22.7%dead ball on BAR-ATH (expected 35–45%)

    Worked

    • Noise with MAD beats the standard deviation in 7 synthetic scenarios out of 7. With 2% outliers the error on σ goes from 22.1 to 0.99.
    • Five rounds of checks without labels corrected as many design flaws (X competing with D, SOFT weights accumulating, minimum duration of D).

    Did not work or stayed open

    • mad_tol (semantic tolerances) discarded: better only in log-loss, worse in Brier in all scenarios.
    • The first D-WHISTLE, based on players slowing down, agrees with the output only 8% of the time.
    • The file is not identical across machines: PC and cloud differ on 1.3% of the frames.
    • Public window 0.411 with 120k training frames and 0.369 with 80k (reduced for laptop memory): the difference is worth one possession, that is noise.
    • Without a validation set no version could be compared with another.
  3. 2
    30/10 · VALIDATION AND v1

    Other matches give a yardstick, and v1 gets worse

    The 7 IDSSE matches are downloaded (Bundesliga, DFL labels) and degraded to resemble the tracker of BAR-ATH. v1 restricts team changes to rule events only, makes the network blind to the team, adds turnovers and makes D-WHISTLE an anchor.

    0.256 / 0.716v1 on degraded IDSSE
    −0.033against v0, worse in 7 matches out of 7
    0.646 → 0.822F1 of D (v0 → v1)
    1117 / 2705possessions predicted / true
    0.345 / 0.741public window, 12 possessions vs 17

    Worked

    • The dead ball: F1 of D from 0.646 to 0.822 and agreement of D-WHISTLE from 8% to 90%.
    • Random-feature network with a convex problem: identical file on every machine (0 frames different in the perturbation tests).

    Did not work or on hold

    • Possessions: v1 merges too much (on BAR-ATH 205 against 329 of v0), and costs 0.033 of spellF1.
    • closed_factor from 20 to 5: 0.261 against 0.256. The gate was not the cause.
    • The random network reproduces 85.5% of the pseudo-labels against 98% of the MLP. Exact integer arithmetic still left 1,896 frames different.
    • Supervised learning on IDSSE (approach A): on hold. Doubt: the tracking of BAR-ATH is dirtier than any degradation and the DFL labels are finer than ours. It will be taken up again on 01/11.
  4. 3
    30/10 · APPROACH C

    Deciding by segments, not by frames

    First the dead ball is drawn, then possession can change only at an observable event (a contact, a turnover, a restart). An exact optimisation picks H or A for each segment with a penalty λ on changes. Version v3 adds the dead ball from the Smart Tagging tags and the goalkeeper window.

    0.316 / 0.734C v2, λ = 0.5
    0.319 / 0.740C v3
    0.459 / 0.802C v2 on the public window, 17 possessions vs 17
    0.512 / 0.830C v3 on the public window
    0.481C v2 with clean tracking

    Worked

    • +0.106 over baseline50 (+50%), +0.027 over v0 in 7 matches out of 7.
    • With clean tracking all versions gain about 0.16: no sign of a system fitted to the dirty data.
    • The dead ball from the tags: D recall +0.042 and spellF1 +0.016 (6/7) against v2.

    Did not work or on hold

    • "Pure" C, with the D of the rules: 0.232 (λ 0.5), 0.223 (λ 1), 0.196 (λ 2). The bottleneck was D.
    • The literal reading of CHALLENGE.md (change only with confirmed control): −0.052 degraded and −0.036 clean, never better in any match. It stays as an option.
    • λ = 0.25 would give 0.323 but chases the finer DFL possessions: 0.5 chosen for caution.
    • D-SP bug in v1 and v2: it marked D until the end of the 35 s tag, inside play. On one match the precision of D fell from 0.94 to 0.81 (spellF1 0.332 → 0.281). With simulated tags v2 is worth 0.303, not 0.316.
    • The goalkeeper window costs 0.004 (degraded) and 0.003 (clean) on IDSSE, but was kept because it was requested.
  5. 4
    30–31/10 · APPROACH B

    A boosted baseline beats all the complicated models

    Closest player with a vote over ±25 frames, the more "open" team when the ball is unseen, and the dead ball of C plus D-SLOW (everyone walking). With B v3 only real controls vote (ball within 1.5 m and under 8 m/s), the team is held for 1 s and a position model decides the rest.

    0.354 / 0.770B
    0.363 / 0.783B v2
    0.473 / 0.822B v3 (+0.110, 7 matches out of 7)
    0.601 / 0.900B v3 clean
    0.506 / 0.836B v3 on the public window

    Worked

    • The oracle showed where the margin was: perfect team 0.812, perfect dead ball 0.442, ball always seen 0.392. Hence the choice to work on the team.
    • The more open team guesses the team in possession on 71% of the frames with the ball unseen, where C reached 60%.
    • D-SLOW: precision 94%, F1 of D from 0.835 to 0.867.

    Did not work or on hold

    • B0 (baseline50 with our D): 0.284, worse than c_v3. Window ±125: 0.29.
    • Mixing "spread" and vote where the ball is seen: at most 0.344. A single score vote plus model: 0.427–0.428.
    • Switched off: early start of D-SLOW (±0.000), corner, throw-in (accuracy −0.009) and goal kick (−0.014 in the wide version).
    • D-BC (broadcast cuts): +6.1% of D, brings D to 48.5%, above the usual 35–45%, and cannot be validated on IDSSE. D-BCN (+1.0%) and the broadcast ball (+7 possessions) give little. All off.
    • Goalkeeper correction and keeper hold: no effect on IDSSE (+0.000), kept for BAR-ATH.
    • On the public window c_v3 (0.513) beats B (0.471): the window and IDSSE do not give the same order.
  6. 5
    01/11 · RULES AND CLEANING

    Errors turned into general rules

    Each error becomes a rule with no frame or slot written in the code, and is accepted only if it improves IDSSE.

    0.4720 → 0.4752R1 + R4 + R5 together
    0.4795rules after R6 and R3c
    1,324 framesreassigned by the goalkeeper cleaning on BAR-ATH (0.83%)
    +0.0039R6, islands of play (7/0)

    Worked

    • Cleaning goalkeeper identity: with 86 simulated swaps it recovers 87% of the loss (−0.011 → −0.001).
    • The dead ball starts when the ball goes out (Law 9): +0.0032 on clean (6/7), and 98–99% of the frames given back to play really are play.
    • R1 (the restart closes the stoppage at the kick) +0.0019, R4 +0.0014, R6 +0.0039, R3c +0.0004.

    Did not work or on hold

    • R2 (relative speed) −0.0025 degraded and −0.0107 clean. R2b (ball that does not slow down) −0.0074 and −0.0132, never better. They fixed cases seen on BAR-ATH but worsened IDSSE: discarded.
    • R3 (unseen ball out) −0.0023: discarded, stays in the code switched off. R1b −0.0017: off.
    • Goalkeeper catch: no measurable gain (0 frames changed on three variants out of four), one correct intervention only. Turned on by the team on 01/11.
    • R5 (team changes in fragments): +0.0094 with simulated swaps, but 4 matches better and 3 worse. It is the least certain.
    • Interpolating the ball in the gaps: under 1 s +0.0013…+0.0017 degraded and −0.0002…−0.0016 clean (off). On gaps of 3–6 s the median error is 3.9 m and only 19% falls within the control radius.
    • On the public window the cleaning brought spellF1 down from 0.506 to 0.471 and macroF1 up from 0.845 to 0.858: noise, not used to decide.
  7. 6
    01/11 · BRANCH SUPERVISED-TEST

    The team is learned from other matches

    The idea put on hold on 30/10 comes back, but only for the team in possession: a symmetric logistic regression with 31 and then 49 features, with a two-state Viterbi on the live frames. The dead ball stays with the rules.

    0.4795 → 0.4977base model (6/1)
    0.5521with "trans" and "paper" (+0.0713 over the rules, 7/0)
    0.6934clean
    0.6815 / 0.8889public window, 10 possessions vs 17
    261 / 317possessions on BAR-ATH, model / rules

    Worked

    • Transitions (the shape of the teams before and after the frame): +0.0498 (7/0).
    • Contacts and duels from the literature: +0.0243 (6/1) on degraded, +0.0532 (7/0) on clean.
    • The gain is 5-10 times that of a single rule.

    Did not work or on hold

    • "edge" group (ball continued for 1 s): −0.0002 and −0.0044, discarded. "inf" group: +0.0001, discarded.
    • Without the change cost (no decoding) the model does much worse than the rules: decoding weighs as much as the classifier.
    • It makes fewer possessions (261 against 317) and the model is off in the default configuration.
  8. 7
    03/11 · H/A/D HMM

    A single model decides possession and stoppages

    The dead ball becomes a learned classifier (40 inputs, BallStatus labels) and a single three-state HMM, with transitions counted on the other six matches, decides H, A and D together.

    0.5895degraded IDSSE (+0.0374, 7/0)
    0.7137clean (+0.0202, 6/1)
    0.5639with simulated team swaps
    0.546transF1@25 (learned team: 0.501)
    0.6194 / 0.8521public window, 14 possessions vs 17

    Worked

    • Learned D: live/dead accuracy 0.942 against 0.914 of the rules on degraded (7/7), 0.981 against 0.971 on clean.
    • Stable result: with w between 0.75 and 1.0 it changes by less than 0.002.

    Did not work or on hold

    • Using the mask of the rules as P(D) the HMM drops to 0.563 (degraded) and 0.696 (clean).
    • Domain gap. On the public window it is below the team model (0.6194 against 0.6815). The F1 of D is 0.750 against 0.822 and on BAR-ATH it writes 39.9% of D against 43.6% of the rules.
    • Interpolating the gaps before the HMM: at most +0.005, not consistent (4/3). Off.
    • The default CSV did not change: hmm3.enabled is false.
  9. 8
    03/11 · THE REFEREE'S WHISTLE

    The audio says where fouls really start

    Fouls and offsides are the hardest stoppages: the ball is inside the pitch and the tracking arrives late. The thread starts on 29/10: D-WHISTLE v0 (8% agreement), then the anchor in v1 (90%), then D-SLOW (everyone walking). On 03/11 the real whistle arrives, detected from the audio of the tactical recording with a two-tone test (3753 Hz and +441 Hz), with no labels and no training.

    95whistles found, 80 on graded frames
    47 / 12 / 11 / 10inside a rule stoppage / stoppage within 3 s / between 3 and 10 s after / no stoppage
    1.8 smedian delay between whistle and rule stoppage
    0.6963 / 0.9109team model + R7a, public window (before 0.6815 / 0.8889)
    0.761 → 0.848recall of D; F1 of D 0.822 → 0.869
    0.438 → 0.545transF1@25

    Worked

    • R7a: a whistle with the ball live starts the stoppage if the players slow down within 3 s. It adds 3,241 frames, 24 stoppages (9 whistles refused because nobody slows down). Those frames are in a broadcast cut 50% of the time, against 17% of the live frames and 68% of the dead ones.
    • D starts on time: mean latency goes from +0.7 to −2.9 frames.
    • It is the highest public score of all versions; macroF1 with bootstrap 5–95% between 0.80 and 0.96.

    Did not work or on hold

    • Never measured on IDSSE: with no audio, simulating it would have meant inventing how it sounds and when it comes. It was a conscious choice, not an omission.
    • With the rules alone R7a raises macroF1 (0.8643 → 0.8856) but lowers spellF1 (0.4706 → 0.4571). Within the noise, but not a clear win.
    • R7b (the whistle inside a stoppage announces the restart): off. It would give back to play 13,717 frames that look as dead as stoppages (cuts 0.71) and with a kick required it acts 6 times.
    • With the HMM the effect of R7a is nil (identical file).
    • Reproducing it needs the audio extracted with ffmpeg and detect_whistles.py. CSV: run_b3_whistle_sup, 253 possessions, D at 45.8%.
  10. 9
    04/11 · PROVISIONAL · SECTIONS 19–22

    What IDSSE can really tell us about BAR-ATH

    A pause to understand how far the results on IDSSE carry over and where the points still to take are. All these numbers use a fallback degradation, so they are provisional until run_idsse_round.ps1 repeats them.

    0.5916 → 0.6117HMM on sd8: trained on degraded / on sd8 (cost of the gap, about 0.02)
    +0.202spellF1 if we added the missing changes (oracle)
    1 in 35-minute windows with the wrong sign for a true gain of 0.03
    308 / 386possessions per match, HMM / true

    Worked

    • The hypothesis "ball loss tied to the game state makes the HMM lose" is rejected: it stays ahead in every scenario, trained on one degradation or the other.
    • The largest margin lies in the changes the model does not find (+0.202), not in their date (±1 s: +0.028; ±2 s: +0.060).
    • R1b becomes positive in the sd5 scenarios (+0.0028, 6/1) and sd8: candidate for the next round.

    Did not work or on hold

    • Refining the date of the changes: +0.0047 (5/2), off and model not saved. Merging fragments: −0.0023 (0/4), discarded.
    • R2 and R2b stay negative in every scenario and also on clean: the most reliable non-gains.
    • The RF-DETR ball detector is ready but not run: question 3 to the organisers is open.
    • The public window cannot see effects of this size: a rule worth 0.005 changes no frame in 67% of the windows.
